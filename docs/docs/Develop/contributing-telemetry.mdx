Langflow no longer sends product analytics to Scarf. This includes startup and shutdown, registered email addresses, flow and component execution, component inputs, deployments, integrations, exceptions, and LFX MCP operations.
No opt-out is required, and no supported setting can re-enable the removed integration.

## Existing deployment settings

`DO_NOT_TRACK`, `LANGFLOW_DO_NOT_TRACK`, and `LANGFLOW_TELEMETRY_BASE_URL` are obsolete for Scarf product analytics.
Existing deployments can keep these settings without preventing startup, but they have no effect on the removed integration. You can remove them from your configuration.
Setting an opt-out variable to `false` or supplying a telemetry URL does not enable analytics.

Registration remains available for Langflow Desktop, and registered email addresses remain in the local registration file. Registration and subsequent startup no longer send an email telemetry event.

## Observability and metering

Customer-configured [OpenTelemetry export](/observability-opentelemetry), Prometheus metrics, and [LLM tracing](/traces) remain available. Configure these through their own settings; the legacy Scarf settings do not control them.
Provider integrations continue to make the requests needed to execute your flows.

Langflow also preserves its bounded, in-process completed-run store for Enterprise metering. Completed runs retain their identifiers, success status, duration, webhook flag, and completion timestamps. Enterprise consumes these records through the existing drain interface.
Removing Scarf does not disable required IBM License Service (LS) or Usage Metering Service (UMS) metering.
